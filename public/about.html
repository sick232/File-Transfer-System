About Instant Share

Instant Share lets you quickly transfer files or text between devices. Upload a file or share text, get a code, and use it to receive the content on another device. All data is deleted after 15 minutes for privacy and security.

  • Files and text are never stored longer than 15 minutes.
  • No account or login required.
  • Works on desktop and mobile browsers.
